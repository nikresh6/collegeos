FORMULA DECODER

What the symbols are saying

Most formulas here ask how attendance or tickets sold (Q) changes when a price or income variable changes. The scary derivative symbol is usually just the slope from the demand equation.

PYour team's ticket price
QTickets sold or attendance
MCCost of one more unit or fan
MRRevenue from one more unit
∂Q/∂PHow Q changes when P rises by 1, holding other things fixed
εElasticity, a percentage sensitivity measure
